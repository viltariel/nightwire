Leprosarium - Amiensus

Bandcamp

Progressive black metal. This track is from the second part of the Reclamation double album - the highlight of the band's work to date.

Reborn to Kill Once More - Stabbing

Bandcamp

Possibly favourite modern brutal death metal band. Although I am generally more drawn to prog/tech death, this album delivers everything I want in brutal death. The vocalist, Bridget Lynch, is excellent.

Bodies - Cwfen

Bandcamp

A new band from Scotland, making their debut with some otherworldly gothic metal/doomgaze. Dark, atmospheric and ethereal.

Instant Results - Changeling

Bandcamp

Another new band, founded by Tom Geldschläger (of Obscura, Belphegor, Defeated Sanity and many more!). They make their debut with this hour-long album of prog/tech death.

Prisoner of the Moon - Wode

Bandcamp

A track from Wode's 2025 album -- stylistically diverse black metal, with influences ranging from '80s heavy metal to gothic doom.

Dissociate - Cairiss

Bandcamp

A full decade after their debut EP, Cairiss have returned with their first album, bringing their ethereal and melancholy style of blackgaze.

The Sinking Isle - Oromet

Bandcamp

Dark, atmospheric funeral doom, both heavy and serene. What stands out to me most is the drumming, and how it intensifies before the calmer passages.

A Lofty Grave - Artificial Brain

Bandcamp

Atmospheric death metal. Somehow both dissonant and melodic, Artificial Brain craft a unique, alien atmosphere. One of those albums that sounds like the cover art.

Myrthys - Uulliata Digir

Bandcamp

Uulliata Digir play a very unique style of avant-garde metal. On their debut album, they combine a primal, ritualistic sound with chaotic metal dissonance and influence from jazz. Vocals range from beautiful melodic cleans to deranged growls and screams. Some of the most original music I've heard in a while.

Legado - Vauruvã

Bandcamp

A track from this unique atmospheric black metal record, with strong influences from progressive rock and Brazilian folk.
